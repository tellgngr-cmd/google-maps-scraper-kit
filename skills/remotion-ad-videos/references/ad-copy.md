# Ad copy

## Length limits (fit the template at readable sizes)
- hook: ≤ 8 words / ~45 characters
- benefits: 2–4 items, each ≤ 5 words / ~30 characters
- proof: ≤ 6 words, ideally with a number
- cta: 2–4 words, starts with a verb

## Hook formulas (the first 2 seconds decide everything)
- Question: "Need 500 dentist leads in Miami?"
- Pain / stop: "Stop buying stale lead lists"
- Number / result: "1,000 roofers' phone numbers in 10 minutes"
- Call-out: "Agency owners: your next clients are on Google Maps"
- Contrast: "Cold lists cost $500. This costs $0."

## Benefits
Outcomes over features: "Export to CSV in minutes" beats "CSV export feature".

## Proof
Specific numbers beat adjectives: "Used by 2,000+ agencies", "4.9★ from 300 reviews".
Only use claims the user has given you or confirmed; don't invent stats. If there's no
real proof, leave `proof` out.

## CTA
Match the funnel stage: cold audience → "Try it free" / "See how"; warm → "Get the kit
today"; retargeting → "Claim 20% off".
